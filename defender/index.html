Safety

Built by breaking it first.

Every rule below exists because something went wrong once. They are built in, not settings someone can switch off on a busy day.

RuleWhat it means for you
Off means offThe switch removes the handset itself. Nothing is left to dial with. Everything stops within 2 minutes.
Their daytime onlyCalls go out 10:00 to 22:00 in the lead's own time zone. Never 3 a.m. in London.
Stops when they answerThe moment a lead picks up or replies, automation stops for that lead.
Your voice, your sendDrafts are drafts. No message leaves without a person pressing the button. WhatsApp stays human.
Unknown is never zeroIf a read fails, it says so. It never shows an empty book as a quiet zero.
One bad lead never stops the runEvery lead is handled on its own. One failure cannot cancel the other four hundred.
