Trading agents · a BRX product

Trading software that runs on your account, under your rules.

You set the rules. The agent checks every trade against 7 gates, applies your risk limits and logs each decision. It starts in simulation. You decide if it ever trades for real.

See the plansGet the free whitepaper

What it does

  1. Runs your rulesEntries, exits and position size come from rules you set and can read.
  2. 7 gates per tradeA trade that fails one gate does not happen.
  3. Risk railsDaily loss limits and position caps stop it on its own.
  4. A journal of every decisionOn your dashboard and in your Telegram, trade by trade.

What it is not

  1. Not a fundWe never hold your money, your account or your keys.
  2. Not adviceIt runs the rules you choose. It does not tell you what to buy.
  3. No return promisesTests show how the rules behaved in the past, not what you will earn.
  4. Your own brokerIt connects to your account. You can switch it off at any time.
