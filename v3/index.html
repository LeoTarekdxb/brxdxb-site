The leak · 02

Where the deals actually go.

01

They cool overnight

A portal enquiry lands at 23:00. An agent opens it at 09:00. By then a faster desk has already called, and a real buyer looks like a dead lead. Nothing was wrong with the lead — only the hours.

02

The language is wrong

An Arabic, Russian or Chinese buyer receives a generic English message. It reads like a blast, so it is ignored, and the file is closed on someone who was ready to transact.

03

The second touch never happens

The follow-up two days later is the one that converts. It goes out when a person remembers it — and your best agents are on calls, not watching a queue. The pipeline leaks quietly, every day.

The sequence · 03

Four things, in this order, every night.

01 Answers

Within minutes, at any hour

Every new enquiry in your CRM gets a personal reply in under five minutes, in the buyer's own language — 03:00 or 15:00, weekend or holiday.

02 Scores

Ranks who is worth the call

Each lead is scored on stated intent and budget from the reply it gets back, so the morning starts with the handful that can close rather than the whole queue.

03 Follows up

Keeps the thread alive

The follow-up goes out on schedule, in the right language, whether or not anyone remembered it — and stops the moment a human takes over the conversation.

04 Briefs you

Before you open your laptop

One briefing each morning: what came in overnight, what was answered, who is worth calling first. Full visibility, nothing for your agents to learn.
