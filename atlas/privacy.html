Privacy policy

BRX Atlas iPhone app · BRX, Dubai · last updated 13 October 2026

What the app stores

Your BRX Atlas sign-in key, in the iPhone Keychain, and the last buildings you opened, on the device. Nothing else.

What the app sends

Only the requests needed to load the atlas from our servers over HTTPS. No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no third-party SDKs.

Owner information

Ownership records come from the public Dubai Land Department register. Contact details are shown only to verified real-estate professionals whose key has been granted access, and never to the public. Owners can ask for their record to be hidden by writing to leo@brxdxb.com.

Face ID

Face ID is evaluated by iOS on the device. The app never receives biometric data.

Deleting your data

Sign out in the app to remove the key from the Keychain. Email us to revoke a key or delete an account.
