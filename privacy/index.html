What we collect

  • What you type when you sign up: name, mobile, email, company, role and how you heard about us.
  • Your login: a 6-digit email code, and a password only if you set one. We store passwords hashed, never in plain text.
  • What you use: the listing links you paste and the buildings you open, so the product works and we can fix it.
  • Basic technical data: IP address and browser, used for security and rate limits.

What the product shows

BRX Atlas shows Dubai building and unit data and DLD-registered sales. Subscribers do not see owner names, owner phone numbers or owner emails.

Why we use your data

  • To create your account, send your code and run your 7-day trial.
  • To contact you about your account or trial. We send no marketing lists, and you can ask us to stop at any time.
  • To keep the service secure and working.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your data. We use service providers to run the product: Cloudflare (hosting and security), Stripe (payments, only if you subscribe), Apple (only if you use the iPhone app) and email providers to deliver your code. They process data only to provide their service.

How long we keep it

  • We keep your account data while your account or trial is open.
  • When a trial ends without a subscription, we delete the data on request, and in any case within 12 months.
  • A paying account: kept while you subscribe, plus the period the law requires for invoices.
  • Security logs: kept only as long as needed to protect the service.

Your rights

Under the UAE Personal Data Protection Law you can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or to stop us using it. Email leanderkhatib@gmail.com and we will answer within 30 days.

Cookies

We use one login cookie to keep you signed in, and a basic Cloudflare analytics beacon with no ad tracking.
