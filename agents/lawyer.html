Example · a 6-lawyer firm

An inquiry at 22:10. A signed engagement letter by noon.

  1. 22:10A WhatsApp inquiry arrives in Arabic about a rental dispute.
  2. 22:12The agent asks 5 intake questions and runs the conflict list. Clear.
  3. 22:15It drafts the engagement letter from the firm's template.
  4. 09:30A lawyer reviews, edits one line and sends it.
  5. 11:40Signed. The hearing date is in the calendar with reminders.

The agent never gives an opinion on the case. It collects, drafts and reminds.
