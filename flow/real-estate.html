How it works

Four parts. Your agent
stays in charge.

  1. 01

    Your CRM

    We read new leads and their timers from the CRM you already use. Nothing to migrate, nothing to retrain.

  2. 02

    The switch

    One switch on a screen on your desk. ON, the system works your leads. OFF, every call, text and email stops within 2 minutes.

  3. 03

    The handset

    Calls go out from a company handset on your desk — never an agent's personal number — and only in the lead's daytime.

  4. 04

    The agent

    The moment a lead picks up or replies, the system steps back and your agent takes the conversation. Humans close.

Safety by design

Rules your leads never have
to ask for.

Aggressive automation burns numbers and brands. These limits are built in, not settings someone can switch off on a busy day.

RuleWhat it means for your leads
Daily capsAt most 25 texts and 40 emails a day per desk. No blasts.
Two touches, then stopA lead gets two automated touches. After that, only a human decides what happens next.
Stop on contactThe moment a lead picks up or replies, automation stops for that lead.
10:00–22:00 local onlyCalls and texts only in the lead's own daytime, by their time zone — never at 3 a.m. in London.
Respect the no“Stop”, “not interested” or an unsubscribe ends all contact with that lead. For good.
No WhatsApp automationWhatsApp stays human. No bots, no bulk sends, no risk to your numbers.
Off means offFlip the switch and every outbound action stops within 2 minutes.
Nothing hiddenEvery call, text and email is logged where you can read it. You see exactly what went out, and when.
